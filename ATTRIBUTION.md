# Attribution

Every map in this repository is built from the data below. Use the credit line printed in the
map's `manifest.json` (`credit_line`) when you reuse it.

## OpenStreetMap

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Data available under the Open Database License:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

## Overture Maps Foundation

Building heights that OpenStreetMap lacks come from Overture Maps buildings. Overture's attribution
requirements: https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution

The Overture features used cite their sources in each building's `overture_sources`. Where a source
is one of these, its own credit applies too (wording from Overture's attribution page):

- **Esri Community Maps:** Esri Community Maps contributors. Available under CC BY 4.0.
- **Google Open Buildings:** Google Open Buildings. Available under CC BY 4.0.
- **Microsoft ML Buildings:** Global ML Building Footprints. Licensed by Microsoft under the Open
  Database License.
- **Other contributors** Overture lists for buildings: USGS 3D Elevation Program data, East Asian
  building vectors (CC BY 4.0) and Spanish BTN 2024 data (CC BY 4.0).

CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## Not included

Terrain and elevation data (AWS Terrain Tiles) is not part of this database and is not published
here.
